Points have no cash value

Pools in Burna run on points. Points have no cash value. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred, or exchanged for money, gift cards or anything of value, inside or outside the app.

How pools work

  • You spend points (50, 100 or 200, set by the pool) to join a pool. Each pool has a calorie goal and a scoring window. The person who creates a pool joins it free.
  • Only qualifying workouts recorded in Apple Health during the scoring window count.
  • When a pool ends, everyone who reached the calorie goal shares the pot of points equally. Players who did not reach the goal lose the points they put in. If nobody reaches the goal, the points are not paid out to anyone.
  • The creator of a pool receives 10% of its pot, in points, when the pool ends.
  • Your rank in the app follows your points balance and can go up or down.
  • A pool with too few players may be cancelled, and entry points returned.
