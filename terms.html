How the game works

  • Each week runs from Sunday 3 AM to the next Sunday 3 AM, Pacific time. Join the week from the app to appear on its leaderboard.
  • Only workouts recorded in Apple Health during the week count. Each workout earns effort, and your effort adds up over the week.
  • When the week ends, your place on the leaderboard moves your rating, and your rating sets your rank. Your rank can go up or down.
  • We may change how effort, ratings and ranks are worked out to keep the game fair.

Usernames, reports and blocking

Your username and the name on your profile are shown to other players, so keep them clean: nothing hateful, sexual, abusive, threatening, impersonating or spam, and nothing that shares someone else's private information. To report a player, open their profile, tap the menu and choose Report. For anything else, use Support in the app menu or email support@burna.win. Reports go only to the Burna team. You can also block any player from the same menu. We review reports promptly, and we may remove content, remove results or suspend or remove accounts that break these rules.
