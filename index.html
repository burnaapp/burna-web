01

OPEN. CONNECT. GO.

Sign in with Apple and connect Apple Health. Your workouts sync automatically, from Apple Watch or any fitness app that saves to Health. Burna only reads your workouts. It never writes to Health.

02

PICK A LEAGUE.

Join the weekly 2K or 5K league, or start one with friends. The entry is 10 or 30 points, and everyone chases the same calorie goal over the same week. New accounts start with points on us.

03

MOVE. IT ALL COUNTS.

Runs, rides, classes, lifting. Every real workout after the league starts adds to your total. Watch the bar fill and see exactly how many calories a day you need to finish.

04

WIN THE POT.

The leaderboard updates as you and your friends train. When the league ends, everyone who hit the goal splits the pot. Miss it and your entry is gone. Your rank rises and falls with your points.

05

RANK UP.

Your rank follows your points. Win a league and watch it climb, from RECRUIT all the way to BURNA. Collect badges on the way and share your new rank with one tap. Run low on points? Free daily challenges top you back up.

FAIR BY DESIGN

Scores come straight from real Apple Health workouts. Hand-typed workouts do not count, and any workout that looks off can be reported.

HOST YOUR OWN

Start a league with friends, share an invite code, and keep 10% of the pot in points. You join free.

DAILY CHALLENGES

Clear free daily challenges for bonus points and top your balance back up between leagues.

NO CASH, NO CATCH

Leagues run on points. Points can't be bought, sold or cashed out. Show off your rank, not your wallet.
