01

OPEN. CONNECT. GO.

Sign in with Apple, connect Apple Health, and you're in. Calshi only reads your workouts. It never writes to Health.

02

PICK A POOL.

Choose a pool and put in the entry points. Everyone in the pool chases the same calorie goal over the same few weeks. New accounts start with 100 points on us.

03

MOVE. IT ALL COUNTS.

Runs, rides, classes, lifting. Every workout after the pool starts adds to your total. Watch the bar fill and see exactly how many calories a day you need to finish.

04

CLIMB. COLLECT.

The leaderboard updates as you and your friends train. When the pool ends, the top finishers split the pot of points.

FAIR BY DESIGN

Scores come straight from Apple Health workouts, with a dispute button for anything that looks off.

MADE FOR FRIENDS

Share an invite code and start a pool together, or join an official one.

NO CASH, NO CATCH

Pools run on points. Points have no cash value and can't be cashed out.
