01

OPEN. CONNECT. GO.

Sign in with Apple, Google or email, pick a username and connect Apple Health. Your workouts sync automatically, from Apple Watch or any fitness app that saves to Health. Burna only reads your workouts. It never writes to Health.

02

JOIN THE WEEK.

A new leaderboard starts every Sunday at 3 AM Pacific and runs for seven days. Join with one tap and you are up against everyone playing that week. Free, every week.

03

MOVE. IT ALL COUNTS.

Runs, rides, classes, lifting. Every real workout earns effort, worked out on your phone from the calories you burn and your body weight, so bigger and smaller players compete on even terms. Your body weight never leaves your phone.

04

CLIMB THE BOARD.

Your effort adds up all week and the leaderboard moves as everyone trains. Pass the player above you before Sunday morning, when the week locks and a fresh one begins.

05

RANK UP.

Where you finish each week moves your rank. Every rank has five steps, from ROOKIE I all the way to BURNA. Finish high to climb. Slip down the board and you can drop back.

  1. ROOKIE
  2. MOVER
  3. CHARGER
  4. GRINDER
  5. ATHLETE
  6. BEAST
  7. BURNA

06

BRING YOUR FRIENDS.

Follow friends, see their latest workouts and find them on the board. Rather keep your workouts to yourself? Turn off Share my workouts and they stay private.

FAIR BY DESIGN

Effort comes straight from real Apple Health workouts and is scaled to body weight. Hand-typed workouts do not count, and any player who looks off can be reported.

A FRESH START WEEKLY

Every Sunday at 3 AM Pacific the board resets. A slow week is behind you the moment the next one starts.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL

Your body weight stays on your phone. Hide your workouts from everyone with one switch, and block anyone you like.

NO CASH, NO CATCH

Burna is free. Nothing to buy, no money and no prizes. Show off your rank, not your wallet.
