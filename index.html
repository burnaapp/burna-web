01

OPEN. CONNECT. GO.

Sign in with Apple, connect Apple Health, and you're in. Burna only reads your workouts. It never writes to Health.

02

PICK A POOL.

Choose a pool and put in the entry: 50, 100 or 200 points. Everyone chases the same calorie goal over the same few weeks. New accounts start with 100 points on us.

03

MOVE. IT ALL COUNTS.

Runs, rides, classes, lifting. Every workout after the pool starts adds to your total. Watch the bar fill and see exactly how many calories a day you need to finish.

04

WIN THE POT.

The leaderboard updates as you and your friends train. When the pool ends, everyone who hit the goal splits the pot. Miss it and your entry is gone. Your rank rises and falls with your points.

05

RANK UP.

Your rank follows your points. Win a pool and watch it climb, from RECRUIT all the way to BURNA. Collect badges on the way and share your new rank with one tap. Run low on points? Free daily challenges top you back up.
