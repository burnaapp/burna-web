  • 7 daysSunday 3 AM to the next Sunday 3 AM, Pacific time.

  • FreeNothing to buy, no money and no prizes. You play for your rank.

How it works

Every workout counts.

  1. 01

    Work out your way

    Runs, rides, classes, lifting. Every workout you record in Apple Health, from Apple Watch or any app that saves to Health, earns effort. Burna only reads your workouts. It never writes to Health.

  2. 02

    Play the week

    A new leaderboard starts every Sunday at 3 AM Pacific and runs for seven days. Join with one tap, and your effort adds up all week as everyone trains.

  3. 03

    Climb the ranks

    When the week locks, where you finish moves your rank. Finish high to climb. Slip down the board and you can drop back.

Effort

Worked out on your phone. Only the score is sent.

  • Harder scores more

    Work harder and you score more, whatever your size.

  • Added up all week

Friends

Bring your friends.

Follow friends, see their latest workouts and find them on the board. Rather keep your workouts to yourself? Turn off Share my workouts and they stay private.

  • Find anyone

    Search by username and add them. Following is open, so they can add you back.

  • See what they did

    Each friend shows their rank and, when they share it, their latest workout: ran two hours ago, lifted yesterday.

  • Friends on the board

    Switch the leaderboard from Global to Friends and it is just you and the people you follow.

Privacy

Your body weight never leaves your phone.

  • Only effort is sent

    Your phone reads your body weight, distance and heart rate to work out each workout’s effort. Only the effort score goes to our server.

  • Read only

    Burna reads your workouts from Apple Health. It never writes to Health, and you can turn access off any time.

  • Your workouts, your call

    Turn off Share my workouts and they are hidden from everyone. Block anyone you like; they are not told.

  • No ads, no tracking

    We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or track you across other apps and websites.
