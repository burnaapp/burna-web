PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Burna is a weekly fitness game. Your Apple Health workouts earn effort, your weekly effort puts you on a leaderboard with other players, and your place each week moves your rank. You can also follow other players and see their recent workouts. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information. A unique account ID, your email address and how you signed in (Sign in with Apple, Google or email). If Apple or Google shares your name when you first sign in, we keep it as the name on your Burna profile. If you sign up with email and a password, we send you a link to confirm your address.
  • Username. The unique username you choose. Other players see it and can find you by it in search.
  • Workout data from Apple Health. With your permission, Burna reads your workouts and their active calories. For each workout from roughly the last two weeks, the app sends our server its Apple Health workout ID, the type of workout, its start and end time, its active calories, and its effort score. Burna never writes to Apple Health.
  • Health data that stays on your phone. Burna also reads your body weight, workout distance and heart rate from Apple Health. Your phone uses them to work out each workout's effort score and to check that a workout was recorded by a real device. They are not sent to our server: only the effort score is.
  • Game activity. The weeks you play, your effort and place on each week's leaderboard, the workouts counted that week, and your rating and rank.
  • Social activity. Who you follow and who follows you, the players you block, and your Share my workouts setting.
  • Reports. If you report a player or a problem from the app, we receive the reason you pick, what you write, who or what the report is about, and your app version.
  • Notification token. If you allow notifications, a token that lets us send them to your device, with your device platform and app version. We use it only to deliver the notifications you have not turned off, such as workouts recorded, rank-ups, game results, game reminders and new followers.
  • App verification. Each request from the app carries a token from Firebase App Check (using Apple App Attest) that shows it comes from the genuine Burna app on a real device. It holds no personal content, and we use it only to keep the service secure and fair.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, it sends a crash report through Firebase Crashlytics: where in the app it went wrong, your device model, iOS version and app version. Crash reports are not linked to your account, and Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days. We use them only to fix bugs.
  • Basic technical data. Standard logs our hosting provider keeps (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability.

Former waitlist

If you joined the launch waitlist on burna.win before the app was released, we stored your email address and the date you signed up. We use it only to tell you about Burna and to understand interest in the app. We do not sell it or share it. To be removed, email support@burna.win.

How we use it

  • To run the game: sign you in, count your workouts, build each week's leaderboard and update your rank.
  • To run the social features: show your profile and recent workouts to other players, and let you follow and find players.
  • To keep the game and the community fair: review reports and act on cheating and abuse.
  • To send the notifications you have turned on.
  • To keep the service secure and working, including blocking requests that do not come from the real app.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who can see what

Other Burna players can see your username and the name on your profile, your rank, and your place, effort and number of workouts on each week's leaderboard. Any player can follow you and open your profile, which shows your rank, wins and follower counts. Nobody can see your email address, your body weight or any other Apple Health data. Reports go only to the Burna team, never to the person reported.

The setting Share my workouts (Menu) is on by default. While it is on, other players can see your recent workouts and the workouts that counted in a week: the type, date and time, length, calories and effort of each. Turn it off and your workouts are hidden from everyone; your name, rank and leaderboard place stay visible.

You can block a player. They can no longer follow you or see your profile, and any follow between you ends in both directions. Blocked players are not told.

Your profile link (burna.win/u/ followed by a short code) can be shared anywhere. The page behind it shows only the name on your profile, and the link opens your profile in the app. People who are not signed in to Burna cannot see anything else.

Services we rely on

Burna runs on Google Cloud and Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, hosted in the United States). Notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging and the Apple Push Notification service, app verification uses Firebase App Check with Apple App Attest, and crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics. Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In are provided by Apple and Google. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. The app sends your recent workouts again each time it syncs, and a workout you delete from Apple Health is removed from that list too. You can delete your account from inside the app (Menu, then Delete account). This removes your sign-in account, your profile and username, the workouts the app uploaded, your follows, followers and blocks, the reports you sent and the reports about you, your notification tokens and your inbox. Results of weeks that have already finished, which other players' past leaderboards are built from, may remain in our records. You can also email us at support@burna.win.

Your choices

  • Turn off Apple Health access any time in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Health.
  • Hide your workouts from other players with Share my workouts, and block anyone you do not want following you.
  • Turn off any group of notifications in the app's notification settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing us.

Children

Burna is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
