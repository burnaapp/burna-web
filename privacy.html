PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Burna is a weekly fitness game. Your Apple Health workouts earn effort, your weekly effort puts you on one leaderboard with the other players, and your place each week moves your rank. You can also add friends and see their latest workout. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information. A unique account ID, your email address and how you signed in (Sign in with Apple, Google or email). Burna does not ask Apple for your name. If you sign in with Google, your Google name may be on your sign-in record, but Burna never uses or shows it, and other players never see it. If you sign up with email and a password, we send you a link to confirm your address.
  • Username. The unique username you choose. It is the only name other players see, and they can find you by it in search.
  • Workout data from Apple Health. With your permission, Burna reads your workouts and their active calories. For each workout from roughly the last two weeks, the app sends our server its Apple Health workout ID, the type of workout, its start and end time, its active calories, and its effort score. Burna never writes to Apple Health.
  • Health data that stays on your phone. Burna also reads your body weight, workout distance and heart rate from Apple Health. Your phone uses them to work out each workout's effort score and to check that a workout was recorded by a real device. They are not sent to our server: only the effort score is.
  • Game activity. The weeks you join, your effort and place on each week's leaderboard, the workouts counted that week, and your rating, rank, level and XP. This also includes the titles you earn and the one you wear.
  • Social activity. Your friends, the friend requests you send and receive, the players you block, and your Share my workouts setting.
  • Reports. If you report a player or a problem from the app, we receive the reason you pick, what you write, who or what the report is about, and your app version.
  • Notification token. If you allow notifications, a token that lets us send them to your device, with your device platform and app version. We use it only to deliver the notifications you have not turned off, such as friend requests, week results and reminders.
  • App verification. Each request from the app carries a token from Firebase App Check (using Apple App Attest) that shows it comes from the genuine Burna app on a real device. It holds no personal content, and we use it only to keep the service secure and fair.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, it sends a crash report through Firebase Crashlytics: where in the app it went wrong, your device model, iOS version and app version. Crash reports are not linked to your account, and Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days. We use them only to fix bugs.
  • Basic technical data. Standard logs our hosting provider keeps (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To run the game: sign you in, count your workouts, build each week's leaderboard and update your rank.
  • To run the social features: show your username, rank and leaderboard results to other players, let you send and accept friend requests, and show your latest workout to your friends.
  • To keep the game and the community fair: review reports and act on cheating and abuse.
  • To send the notifications you have turned on.
  • To keep the service secure and working, including blocking requests that do not come from the real app.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who can see what

Other Burna players see you by username only. They can see your username, your rank, level and worn title, and your place, effort and number of workouts on each week's leaderboard. Nobody can see your email address, your body weight or any other Apple Health data. Reports go only to the Burna team, never to the person reported.

Friends are mutual. You send a friend request and the other player accepts it, or you accept theirs. Either of you can end the friendship at any time, and the other player is not told. Your friends can see your latest workout while Share my workouts is on. There is no following, and Burna has no profile pages or profile links.

The setting Share my workouts (Menu) is on by default. While it is on, other players can see the workouts that counted in a week on the leaderboard (the type, date and time, length, calories and effort of each), and your friends can see your latest workout. Turn it off and your workouts are hidden from everyone; your username, rank and leaderboard place stay visible.

You can block a player. Any friendship or request between you ends, and neither of you can send the other a request or find the other in search. Blocked players are not told.

Services we rely on

Burna runs on Google Cloud and Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, hosted in the United States). Notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging and the Apple Push Notification service, app verification uses Firebase App Check with Apple App Attest, and crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics. Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In are provided by Apple and Google. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. The app sends your recent workouts again each time it syncs, and our server keeps those uploaded workouts for up to 35 days, and a workout you delete from Apple Health is removed from that list too. You can delete your account from inside the app (Menu, then Delete account). This removes your sign-in account, your profile and username, the workouts the app uploaded, the workouts counted in your weeks, your rating, rank, level and titles, your friends, friend requests and blocks, the reports you sent and the reports about you, your notification tokens and your inbox. Results of weeks that have already finished stay in our records so that other players' places and past leaderboards do not change, but they are no longer linked to you: they carry no username or account ID, and they appear as DELETED PLAYER. A week that has not finished yet is worked out without you. You can also email us at support@burna.win.

Your choices

  • Turn off Apple Health access any time in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Health.
  • Hide your workouts from other players with Share my workouts, and block anyone you do not want to hear from.
  • Turn off any group of notifications in the app's notification settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing us.

Children

Burna is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
