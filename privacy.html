PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Burna is a calorie challenge app. You join leagues, your workouts count toward a calorie goal, and you compete on a leaderboard. You can also follow other players and share posts. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information. Your name and email address, and a unique account ID, from Sign in with Apple, Google or email sign-in. If you sign up with email and a password, we send you a link to confirm your address.
  • Workout data from Apple Health. With your permission, Burna reads workouts (type, start and end time, and active calories) to score the leagues you have joined. Burna never writes to Apple Health. For each league, we send the server your calorie total and each workout that counts toward it: its type, start and end time, and active calories. We never send heart rate, routes, or any other Health data.
  • Social activity. Who you follow and who follows you, and the players you block. The posts and comments you write, and the kudos you give. A post can include one of your recent workouts (its type, length and calories) if you choose to add it.
  • Latest workout. Your most recent workout (its type, time and calories), kept so followers can see your status. Only workouts from the last day or two are shown.
  • Reports. If you report a league, a player, a workout, a post, a comment or a problem from the app, we receive what you write, what the report is about (including a copy of the reported text), and your app version.
  • League activity. The leagues you join or create, your calorie totals, your rank, and your points balance and history.
  • Notification token. If you allow notifications, a token that lets us send them to your device. We use it only to deliver notifications you have not turned off, including the ones about new followers, kudos and comments.
  • App verification. Each request from the app carries a token from Firebase App Check (using Apple App Attest) that shows it comes from the genuine Burna app on a real device. It holds no personal content, and we use it only to keep the service secure and fair.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, it sends a crash report through Firebase Crashlytics: where in the app it went wrong, your device model, iOS version and app version. Crash reports are not linked to your account, and Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days. We use them only to fix bugs.
  • Basic technical data. Standard logs our hosting provider keeps (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist on burna.win, we store your email address and the date you signed up. We use it only to tell you when Burna launches and to understand interest in the app. We do not sell it or share it. To be removed, email support@burna.win.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, score leagues, show leaderboards and update your points.
  • To run the social features: show your profile, status and posts to other players, and build each player's feed from the people they follow.
  • To keep contests and the community fair: review reports and act on abuse.
  • To keep the service secure and working, including blocking requests that do not come from the real app.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who can see what

Other players in a league can see your display name and your calorie total for that league. They can also see the workouts that count toward that league: the date, the type of workout, and its calories. Nobody can see your other Apple Health data or your email address. Reports go only to the Burna team, never to the person reported.

Profiles, the feed and sharing

Burna is open follow: any player can follow you and view your profile without asking. Your profile shows your display name, rank, points, wins, follower and following counts, and your recent workouts (type, date, length and calories). Your latest workout shows as your status. Posts, kudos and comments show your display name and are visible to other Burna players, and your posts appear in your followers' feeds.

The setting Share my workouts and status (Profile menu, Privacy) is on by default. Turn it off and your workouts and status are hidden from other players. Your name, rank and the posts you write stay visible, and a workout you attach to a post is shown with that post.

You can block a player. They can no longer follow you or see your profile, any follow between you ends in both directions, and you do not see each other in feeds or comments. Blocked players are not told. You can delete your own posts and comments, and the owner of a post can delete comments on it. You can report any post, comment or player from the app.

Your profile link (burna.win/u/ followed by a short code) can be shared anywhere. The page behind it shows only your display name, and the link opens your profile in the app. People who are not signed in to Burna cannot see anything else.

Services we rely on

Burna runs on Google Cloud and Firebase (authentication and database, hosted in the United States). Notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging and the Apple Push Notification service, app verification uses Firebase App Check with Apple App Attest, and crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics. Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In are provided by Apple and Google. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account from inside the app (Profile, then Delete account). This removes your profile, points, league participation, shared workouts, follows and followers, blocks, posts, comments, kudos and the reports you sent from our systems. Some league results may remain in anonymous form so other players' leaderboards stay correct. You can also email us at support@burna.win.

Your choices

  • Turn off Apple Health access any time in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Health.
  • Hide your workouts and status from other players with Share my workouts and status, and block anyone you do not want following you.
  • Turn off notifications about followers, kudos and comments in the app's notification settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing us.

Children

Burna is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
