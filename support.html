SUPPORT

We usually reply within two business days.

In the app, open the Menu, tap Support and send us a message. Or email support@burna.win with your username and what you were doing.

When does the week start and end?

Each week runs from Sunday 3 AM to the next Sunday 3 AM, Pacific time. Once you have joined, the leaderboard shows how long is left.

How do I join?

Tap join on the home screen. Everyone who joins is on one global leaderboard for the week. You can join at any time during the week, and every workout of the week still counts.

How is effort worked out?

Every workout in Apple Health earns effort, worked out on your phone from the calories you burn, how long you train and your body weight. Your effort adds up over the week. A smaller and a bigger player doing the same work score about the same. Your body weight never leaves your phone.

How do ranks work?

Your rank comes from a rating that moves with where you place each week. Every rank has five steps, shown as Roman numerals, from ROOKIE up to BURNA, and the next rank comes after the fifth step.

What are levels and titles?

You earn XP for every week you play, and XP raises your level. Titles unlock as you level up and reach goals, and you can wear one next to your name. Ranks, levels and titles are only for show. They never change your score.

My workouts aren't showing

Check that Burna can read your workouts: open the Menu, tap Apple Health access, and make sure everything is on. Then open Burna again so it syncs. Only workouts recorded during the week count toward it.

How do friends work?

Search for a username in Friends and tap Add friend. They get a request, and you become friends when they accept it. Either of you can unfriend or block the other later. Friends see your latest workout and show up when you switch the leaderboard to Friends.

Who can see my workouts?

Other players can open your workouts from the leaderboard, and your friends see your latest one in Friends. To keep them to yourself, turn off Share my workouts in the Menu. Others then see a lock. Your username, rank and place on the leaderboard stay visible.

Someone is cheating or has an offensive name

Open the menu next to them in Friends, or tap the report button on their workouts, and choose Report. Reports go to the Burna team, not to the person you report. We review every report, and we remove results and suspend accounts that break the rules.

How do I block someone?

Open the menu next to them in Friends and choose Block. Any friendship or request between you ends, and neither of you can send the other a request. You can unblock them under Blocked players in the Menu.

Can I win money or prizes?

No. Burna is a free game with nothing to buy and no money or prizes. You play for your rank.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Menu and tap Delete account at the bottom. It removes your profile, username, workouts, friends and rank. Results of weeks that have finished stay on the leaderboards, but they are no longer linked to you and show as DELETED PLAYER. Or email us and we'll do it for you.
